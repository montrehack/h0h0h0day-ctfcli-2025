S.P.E.W. — A Brief Manifesto

The Society for the Promotion of Elfish Welfare (S.P.E.W.) was an organisation founded in 1994 by Hermione Granger in response to what she saw as gross injustice in the treatment of house-elves at the 1994 Quidditch World Cup. House-elves were often forced into servitude and denied basic freedoms; S.P.E.W. sought to highlight that unfairness, advocate for humane treatment, and raise awareness about the systemic nature of elven slavery. This manifesto stands against forced servitude and for the dignity and rights of house-elves everywhere.
