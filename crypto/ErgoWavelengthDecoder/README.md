While protecting the citizens of Krat, Carlo's puppet found a strange device.
He brings it to the Genius of Technology, Lorenzini Venigni, and together they discover
it is an Ergo Wavelength Decoder.

With it, they could save the people of Krat from the murderous puppets
but first, they must recover the key used to decode their communications.

Can you help them uncover the key and save the city of Krat?
